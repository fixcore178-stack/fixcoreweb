# FixCore repair page decisions

Reading: a local repair website for customers in Trinidad, with a practical workshop style.
Dials: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Preserve the owner's local headline, logo, business facts, pricing, services and quote flow to retain the established identity.
- Keep cyan for primary actions and section emphasis because it is the existing brand accent; reserve green for WhatsApp actions.
- Retain Sora headings for their compact, sturdy geometry and Geist body text for readable repair descriptions; use body type in navigation and mono only for specifications.
- Use an asymmetric hero with a layered physical phone illustration to connect the repair service to the parts inside a device, without fabricated diagnostic results.
- Render the illustration in CSS so it stays sharp, loads without a new image or 3D runtime, and works without JavaScript.
- Use metallic shading and contact shadows only on physical device layers to communicate depth and materials.
- Put address and hours in a compact strip so practical visit information stays visible without competing with the main message.
- Make the repair quote the primary action because it gathers the information needed for a useful reply; retain direct WhatsApp alongside it.
- Keep the service ledger for comparing repair categories, the parts diagrams for explaining repairs, and the existing form for requesting help.
- Use flat surfaces and section rules instead of a page-wide grid and panel glows to make content hierarchy clearer.
- Keep generous separation between tasks and tighter spacing within related facts so scanning follows the repair journey.
- Display content immediately rather than requiring scroll animations; keep hover, focus and form-state feedback.
- Retain the working light/dark switch so customers can choose their reading preference.
- Keep the illustration explicitly labeled as simplified; do not add invented testimonials, statistics or promises.
